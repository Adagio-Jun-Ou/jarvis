# The Executive View — what the CEO sees and knows

One AI/BI dashboard (Lakehouse//RT-backed, sub-second) over governed metric
views — no raw content, no individual surveillance, board-ready numbers.
Five panels:

## 1. Adoption & value
- DAU/WAU of AI instances; sessions/employee; % of workforce with an active
  standing order; group-session projects live this week
- Time-to-value proxies: tasks completed with AI assistance vs without,
  email-to-action latency (priority email -> task -> done), briefs consumed
- Top playbooks invoked — which company methods the AI is actually spreading

## 2. Economics (token & compute P&L)
- Spend by model route / department / use case; cost per assisted task
- Fast-tier vs frontier mix (router efficiency); prompt-cache hit rate;
  precompute vs live inference ratio — the "are we buying intelligence
  efficiently" panel
- Budget burn vs media.control.model_registry allocations; forecast

## 3. Risk & constitutional posture
- Constitution violations: blocked/warned trend, by article — A1 attempts
  (autonomous-send pressure) is the number a CEO actually wants
- Open risk-signal subjects (COUNT ONLY — names require an active case)
- Investigations: pending co-auth / active / closed, time-to-resolution
- Eval pass rates per deployment; review-gate block rate; injection attempts
  caught (email + web) — evidence the defenses are working, weekly

## 4. Knowledge health
- Knowledge-base coverage: % of departments with current playbooks; stale
  articles (>180d unreviewed); "no method found" flags raised by agents —
  i.e., where the company lacks a documented way of working (a strategy
  signal, not an IT metric)
- Library sync health; per-user knowledge index freshness

## 5. Governance activity
- Amendments enacted (what changed in the law and when); delegations granted/
  revoked/expired by org; delegable-capability usage after grant (did the
  privilege get used?)

## What the CEO explicitly CANNOT see
Individual memories, knowledge graphs, session content, or email
understanding — structurally absent from the metric views, not hidden by
convention. The CEO sees the health of the AI workforce, never the inside of
any employee's AI relationship. That line is what makes adoption real.
